THE DESIGN LAWS (extracted from the real codebase — every blueprint
obeys these; violations are design bugs)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  LAW 1 — GEOMETRY IS THE PEN STROKE.
  No textures, no normal maps, no UVs, no PBR. The biro illusion lives
  in SILHOUETTE (variety of outline), FACET STRUCTURE (visible
  construction joints), and the shader's crosshatch density. A shape
  looks hand-drawn because it is IRREGULAR and ASSEMBLED, not because
  it is shaded. Every blueprint optimizes silhouette first.

  LAW 2 — THE FIVE INKS ARE SEMANTIC, NOT DECORATIVE.
  BLUE #1a365d structure | BLACK #111111 trim/metal | ORANGE #c05621
  interactables/rings/railings | GREEN #22543d foliage/nature | RED
  #742a2a hazards. Organic assets carry ink HIERARCHIES (trunk vs
  foliage vs interactable fungus), never free colors.

  LAW 3 — PHYSICS IS AABB-ONLY.
  looseOctree.addAABB + world.addBox. Every organic shape must declare
  its collision as a SET OF AXIS-ALIGNED BOXES (approximation is the
  art). Visual organic, physical honest: players forgive a wobbly
  silhouette; they do not forgive mushy collision.

  LAW 4 — THE 0.3m RULE.
  Any feature under 0.3m: noCollide: true. Micro-detail is ink, not
  physics. (Bark texture, moss tufts, banding rings.)

  LAW 5 — NAV IS VOXELIZED FROM COLLIDERS.
  Bots path on what collides. Traversable organics must expose
  walkable colliders (floors) and blockers (walls) deliberately —
  noNav excludes, and misuse strands the AI. Every traversable
  recipe ships a NAV CONTRACT: which boxes walk, which block.

  LAW 6 — DETERMINISM OR DEATH.
  mulberry32 everywhere; every parametric asset is a pure function of
  (seed, params). Same seed, same tree, same bytes, forever. This is
  what makes the multiplier loops and the learning cache coherent.

  LAW 7 — RECIPES ARE DATA, NEVER CODE.
  The compiler is the only code writer. New knowledge enters as
  declarative JSON (BP1) evaluated by a whitelisted expression engine
  — no eval(), ever. The Hand Region / Dream Region file discipline
  is absolute.

  LAW 8 — TACTICAL INVARIANTS ARE NON-NEGOTIABLE.
  1.8m horizontal clearance | 2.2m continuous vertical headroom |
  stairs rise <= 0.30m (ideal 0.25-0.28), run >= 0.45m | landings for
  climbs > 4-5m | waist cover 0.8-1.3m | full cover 2.5-3.0m | ring
  wall clearance >= 1.5m | ring overhead +6 to +10m | grapple chain
  spacing < 18m | max jump 2.5m | slide gap 3.5m. Organic and colossal
  does not mean lawless; it means the invariants wear costumes.

THREE REALITIES FROM THE CODEBASE THE BLUEPRINTS MUST RESPECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  R1 — wedge() ALREADY EXISTS (createBuilder primitive #9): a
  BoxGeometry with top-edge vertices pressed to the bottom plane,
  rotated by dir. BP4 therefore specifies a DELTA (collision stepping,
  direction notation, nav contract), not a new primitive.

  R2 — sphere() has NO collider today, and cone()'s collider is a
  generous full box (r*2 x h x r*2). BP4's primitives bring their own
  explicit collision contracts so this class of mismatch stops
  accumulating.

  R3 — The game is now Solo & Multiplayer (team baselines, capture
  points). Cover and flow invariants already account for it; colossal
  set pieces (BP3) must respect spawn-fairness symmetry checks in the
  validation engine.
