4.0 — HOW TO READ THIS FILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each primitive gets five deliverables, matching request.md 9.4:
  (a) geometric construction (vertex math),
  (b) collision boundary (the AABB approximation set — LAW 3),
  (c) nav semantics (walkable vs blocking — LAW 5),
  (d) biro ink treatment (LAW 2 + how facets feed the crosshatch),
  (e) AST node type + cost accounting.

Construction guidance uses three.js geometry classes already in the
project's vocabulary (BoxGeometry, CylinderGeometry, LatheGeometry,
IcosahedronGeometry, ExtrudeGeometry). Per request.md discipline: these
are specifications for your team to implement — the math is exact, the
code is yours.

  THE CROSSHATCH PRINCIPLE (applies to all four): the pen shader draws
  hatch lines following surface normals. FACETS (hard normal changes)
  are therefore ink strokes for free: every flat facet boundary reads
  as a deliberate pen line. These primitives are designed to maximize
  facet boundaries — that is their entire aesthetic.

4.1 — hollowCyl(x, y, z, rInner, rOuter, length, opts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  SEMANTICS: an open-ended annular tube lying on axis 'x' or 'z'.
  (x, y, z) = center of the BORE; y = bore center height.

  (a) CONSTRUCTION — LATHE METHOD (native, one geometry):
      Profile: a closed rectangle in (radius, height) space —
        (rInner, -length/2) -> (rOuter, -length/2)
        -> (rOuter, +length/2) -> (rInner, +length/2) -> close
      LatheGeometry(profile, opts.seg ?? 10) around Y produces:
      outer wall + inner wall + two annular rim faces, open ends —
      exactly a tube with thickness, in ONE geometry.
      Then: axis 'x' -> rotateZ(pi/2); axis 'z' -> rotateX(pi/2);
      translate(x, y, z).
      Low seg (8-12) is intentional: wide facets + J2 radial variance
      (BP2) = a hand-rolled tube, not a pipe.
      DEGENERATE MODE (ultra-low budget): opts.staves = true emits the
      6-box approximation from BP2 2.2 instead — same numbers, no
      lathe, for budget-corner cases.

  (b) FLOOR (the walkable chord — separate concern by design):
      opts.floor (default true) emits ONE box:
        top surface at y - rInner + f        (f = opts.f ?? 0.5)
        width = 2 * sqrt(rInner^2 - (rInner - f)^2)   (~2.2m at
                rInner = 1.5, f = 0.5 — the BP2 derivation)
        length = length, thickness 0.3
      The primitive stays orthogonal: tube = wall, floor = walking.

  (c) COLLISION CONTRACT (4-5 AABBs, honest at the walking band):
        floor box     — walkable collider (nav surface)
        ceiling box   — blocker; bottom at y + rInner - 0.3
        2 side walls  — blockers; inner faces at +-
                        (floorChordHalf + 0.05), height 2*rOuter,
                        thickness rOuter - rInner + 0.3
      The side walls sit at the chord the player actually walks,
      slightly generous above it — physical width tracks visual width
      where feet are, and never pinches the 1.8m invariant.
      Rim faces: visual only (thin, LAW 4 adjacent).

  (d) INK: default BK (bark/gunmetal); moss bands via opts.bands = n
      (thin noCollide cyl shells, radius rOuter + 0.06, spaced
      seeded — LAW 4 decoration).

  (e) AST: { type: "hollow_cyl",
             transform: { x, y, z, length },
             params: { rInner, rOuter, axis, floor, f, seg, bands } }
      Cost: 1 geometry call (+1 floor) + 4 colliders.

  ACCEPTANCE: bore measures >= 1.8m wide / >= 2.2m clear at the floor
  band on 50 seeded instantiations; zero NaN; jitter deterministic.

4.2 — wedge(x, y, z, w, h, d, opts) — THE DELTA SPEC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  REALITY (request.md 6.1, primitive #9): wedge EXISTS — BoxGeometry
  with vertices where (X > 0.001 && Y > 0.001) pressed to Y = -h/2,
  normals recomputed, then rotated by dir ('-x', '+z', '-z'; base
  orientation descends toward +x). Its collider is currently the FULL
  box. This blueprint specifies the delta, not a replacement.

  W1 — DIRECTION NOTATION (harmonized):
      Accept '+x' | '-x' | '+z' | '-z' AND the legacy/request forms
      ('x+', 'x-', 'z+', 'z-'); normalize internally. Canonical table
      (base = descends toward +x; each dir remaps by rotation):
        '+x': high edge at -x    '-x': high edge at +x
        '+z': high edge at -z    '-z': high edge at +z

  W2 — COLLISION: STEPPED, NOT SOLID (the important fix):
      The full-box collider creates phantom volume under the high end
      (unwalkable space that looks open) and mis-voxelizes the nav
      grid. Replace with a stepped approximation hugging the slope:
        K = max( ceil(d / 0.5), ceil(h / 0.30) )     (both the run
                                                       and rise limits)
        sub-box i (i = 0..K-1) along the slope axis:
          center at the i-th strip, top at the ramp surface there,
          full width w, strip depth d/K + 0.004 (overlap kills seams)
      Ramps become K honest stair-steps for physics and nav; the
      visual wedge rides on top untouched.
      opts.collider = 'stepped' (default) | 'solid' (buttress roots,
      sloped roofs — where under-space should NOT exist).

  W3 — NAV: stepped boxes are walkable climb colliders (nav reads a
      ramp); 'solid' blocks beneath. Both intentional, both declared.

  COST: 1 geometry call + K colliders (K logged in cost metadata —
  the embedder's budget dial now sees ramp prices).

4.3 — facetedRock(x, y, z, rx, ry, rz, opts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  SEMANTICS: a sketched boulder — irregular polyhedron with large
  FLAT facets. Cover-role aware: the rock knows what it is for.

  (a) CONSTRUCTION (five steps, all deterministic):
      1. Base: IcosahedronGeometry(1, 0) — 12 vertices, 20 faces.
         Subdivision ZERO is mandatory: facets must be large enough
         to read as pen planes.
      2. Non-uniform scale (rx, ry, rz) — slab-like vs. craggy mass.
      3. Radial displacement per unique vertex (position-hash keyed,
         BP2 J1 rule — shared vertices move together or the mesh
         cracks):
             v' = v * (1 + A * hash01(seed, posHash(v)))
             A = opts.amp ?? seeded in [0.25, 0.45]
      4. FACET PLANES (the signature step): choose k = 3..6 seeded
         unit normals n_j; for each, a support plane at distance
         d_j = (0.55..0.85) * meanRadius along n_j; clamp:
             v' = v' - n_j * max(0, dot(v', n_j) - d_j)
         Sequential clamping yields irregular flat facets — the rock
         looks CUT, like a doodled crystal. This is what separates
         facetedRock from a noise blob.
      5. Grounding: any vertex y < 0 -> y = 0 (the Colossal
         Contract's C1, built in).
      Then: toNonIndexed() + computeVertexNormals() — non-indexed so
      every face keeps HARD edges (facet boundaries survive as normal
      discontinuities, which the pen shader renders as line changes).

  (b) COVER CONTRACT (role-aware clamping — measured after
      displacement):
        opts.cover = 'waist'  -> resulting height clamped to 0.8-1.3m
        opts.cover = 'full'   -> clamped to 2.5-3.0m
        opts.cover = 'decor'  -> no clamp, entire rock noCollide
      The generator never has to remember the tactical bands; the
      primitive enforces them (LAW 8 as a type system).

  (c) COLLISION: 1-3 AABBs sized to the DISPLACED bounds + 0.05:
        cover rocks: one inscribed box (~1.4 * rMean wide) — the
        cover contract IS the collider contract
        landmark rocks (ry > 2m): 2-3 stacked/offset boxes
        approximating the mass (octree-friendly, never concave lies)

  (d) INK: default BK (graphite reads as stone under the pen); GR
      override for mossy. Displacement IS the jitter — do not stack
      J1 on top (double-jitter melts facets).

  (e) AST: { type: "faceted_rock",
             params: { rx, ry, rz, facets: k, amp, cover } }
      Cost: 1 geometry call + 1-3 colliders.

  ACCEPTANCE: cover clamps measured in-band; two seeds -> distinct
  silhouette signatures; grounded (no floaters); 60 vertices / 20
  faces after toNonIndexed (facet integrity check).

4.4 — arch(x, y, z, span, height, depth, opts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  SEMANTICS: an arched WALL with a passable opening. (x, y, z) =
  center of the opening at floor level. span = opening width,
  height = apex clear height, depth = wall thickness along the
  passage axis. Passability invariants: span >= 2.4 and height >= 2.6
  (the 1.8/2.2 invariants with corbel-step and margin headroom) —
  enforced at construction, not hoped.

  (a) CONSTRUCTION — THREE STYLES:
      ROMAN (default, segmented — recommended):
        Semicircle of radius R = span/2, center at height
        h_s = height - R (the spring line). Arc parametrization in
        the wall's XY plane:
            P(a) = (R * cos a, h_s + R * sin a),  a in [0, pi]
        N = opts.seg ?? 10 chord segments; segment i spans
        [a_i, a_i+1] with dA = pi/N:
          box: length = 2R * sin(dA/2) + 0.05 (overlap kills seams)
               thickness t = opts.thick ?? 0.5 (radial, outward)
               depth = depth
          rotated by the tangent angle (a_mid - 90 deg) about the
          passage axis
        Plus two flanking pillars: boxes at +-(span/2 + t/2), from
        floor to h_s, depth x t.
        Visible block joints = sketchbook masonry. THE default.
      POINTED (gothic): two circular arcs of radius R' = span/0.9
        centered at +-(span/2 - R'·?) ... construct as two mirrored
        arc chains meeting at the apex; same chording as roman.
      CORBEL (pure axis-aligned fallback): N stepped boxes, each
        cantilevered inward by span/N per step — zero rotations, the
        most hand-stacked look, works with primitive boxes alone.
      SMOOTH (optional variant): THREE.Shape = outer rectangle
        (span + 2t) x (height + t) with the arch opening as a hole
        path; ExtrudeGeometry(shape, depth). One mesh, smooth curve —
        for pipes and metal vaults where joints would be wrong.

  (b) COLLISION: pillar boxes (blockers) + one AABB per arc chord
      segment (blocker, +0.05 inflation — rotated boxes approximate
      honestly). THE OPENING EMITS NO COLLIDERS — the void is the
      product. Corbel steps are their own honest AABBs.

  (c) NAV: pillars and arc block; the opening is open ground (the
      map's floor passes through); nothing noNav unless opts says.

  (d) INK: BL (structure). Roman/corbel joints catch the crosshatch
      at every chord boundary — the arch is a pen-line generator.

  (e) AST: { type: "arch",
             params: { span, height, depth, style, seg, thick } }
      Cost: roman N+2 calls + N+2 colliders; corbel N; smooth 1+1.

  USE SITES: hollow-trunk niche doorways (BP2), cave mouths on
  ravine cliff faces, bridge abutments (corbel style as the span's
  legs — the deck itself is a separate slab recipe).

4.5 — LANDING A PRIMITIVE (the 6-step registration checklist)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. createBuilder: implement + unit-test the construction math.
  2. Nav contract: document walkable vs blocking colliders (LAW 5).
  3. scene-tree-schema.js: register the node type + params.
  4. scene-compiler.js: add the emitter (with cost metadata).
  5. Recipe whitelist (BP1): the prim becomes teachable/usable in
     RecipeResponse parts the moment it lands.
  6. Acceptance tests: NaN guard, invariant measurement (the tables
     above), determinism, silhouette distinctness.

  SUMMARY TABLE:
  PRIM         CALLS   COLLIDERS    NAV                     DEFAULT INK
  -----------  ------  -----------  ----------------------  -----------
  hollowCyl    1(+1)   4-5          floor walks, walls      BK
                                   block
  wedge (delta) 1      K (stepped)  stepped = climb;        BL
                                   solid = block
  facetedRock  1       1-3          blocks (cover box)      BK
  arch         N+2     N+2          opening open, wall      BL
                                   blocks

4.6 — CONSOLIDATED ACCEPTANCE (the quartet as a suite)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  T1  INVARIANTS: 100 seeded instantiations of each primitive pass
      their measured contracts (bore 1.8/2.2; wedge steps rise<=0.30
      run>=0.45-equivalent; rock cover bands; arch opening 2.4/2.6).
  T2  DETERMINISM: seed -> identical geometry bytes, 20 runs each.
  T3  NO PHANTOMS: no collider occupies space the visuals show as
      open (the wedge W2 fix, verified by raycast sampling against
      collider occupancy in the sandbox).
  T4  FACET INTEGRITY: crosshatch-visible normal discontinuities
      exist at the designed boundaries (normal-histogram check) —
      the pen has lines to follow.
  T5  RECIPE-READY: all four appear in the BP1 whitelist and round-
      trip through a sample recipe (teach -> validate -> instantiate
      -> compile) without special cases.
