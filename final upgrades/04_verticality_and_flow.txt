FILE 04 — VERTICALITY & GAMEPLAY FLOW
Answers Dossier Question 3: "Are the 4 skybridges and terraced
ridges sufficient, or should we add secondary mid-canopy boughs,
root ramps, and grapple swings?"
=====================================================================

THE DIAGNOSIS
-------------
Plot your walkable surfaces by height and a brutal hole appears:

  Y 31.0  apex swing ring                    (1 point, grapple-only)
  Y 22-18  airway rings                      (grapple-only)
  Y 14-12  banyan pickup, conifer grapples   (grapple-only)
  Y  9.5  deck highway: treehouse + 4 bridges + 4 bastions   ✔ built
  Y  3.5-5  ** CONCEPT PROMISES THIS TIER — ALMOST NOTHING BUILT **
  Y  0     ground                            ✔ built

The middle is missing in TWO ways:
1. Tier 1 (3.5-5m) exists in the concept doc ("mantleable rock
   berms, curved log rooftops, stream footbridges") but barely in
   geometry. Ground players have essentially ONE floor.
2. Between ground and deck there is no PLAY-space — only vertical
   transport (stairs/ridges). You cannot FIGHT on the way up.

An arena shooter with a 9.5m dead layer wastes its own map. Here is
the full vertical plan.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 1 — THE 2-UP-1-DOWN CONTRACT (every elevated position)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Any platform above Y=3 must offer:
  - 2+ independent ways UP (so a blocked/broken route never traps
    the tier),
  - at least 1 fast way DOWN (drop, slide, or grapple release),
  - and no route may pass through a single choke narrower than the
    anti-pinch 1.8m.
Audit today:
  - Treehouse deck: stairs + west egress slab + 5 grapples + drop
    from hatch edge → PASSES (this is why it feels good).
  - Bastion decks: 1 bridge + 1 ridge → exactly 2, but after File 01
    fixes the ridge directions, VERIFY each of the 4 actually
    connects (N and S do; E/W ridges were climbing sideways).
  - Giant grass grapple rings: 1 way up (grapple), drop = down →
    OK for a perch, but nothing links them. See Rule 4.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 2 — BUILD TIER 1 (the 3.5-5m meso layer)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Five cheap builders create the missing middle:

  a) BOULDER STACK SHELVES: 2 faceted rocks (1.2m + 0.9m) side by
     side = a 2-step natural mantle to a 3.6m vantage. Place 6-8
     near sector borders (they also serve File 03's blend belts).
  b) LOG-ROOF ROUTES: buildCurvedHollowLog already has a top
     catwalk at ~4m with shelf-stairs — you built the asset and
     never connected it. Add a stepping boulder so the shelf-stairs
     are actually reachable, and point the catwalk's grapple ring
     chain at the NW bastion.
  c) STREAM FOOTBRIDGE (3.5-4m arched): your current footbridge is
     ground-level. Add ONE higher arched crossing upstream (z≈24,
     beside the second stepping stones): arch of 5 slab segments,
     apex 3.8m, with a wedge at each end. It gives the SE quadrant
     a Tier-1 vantage over the glade AND reads beautifully.
  d) FALLEN LEVIATHAN VERTEBRA: one giant disc (r=1.6, h=1.2) lying
     on its side at the skeleton tunnel's north exit + one at 2.4m
     leaning on it = free mantle steps matching the story.
  e) STUMP+MUSHROOM STEP: buildHollowStump (1.3m) next to a
     toadstool cap (2.4m) next to giant grass (grapple at 7m) —
     a 3-beat "vertical sentence" teaching the ascent language.
     Place this trio pattern in all 4 quadrants (vary rotation).

Result: a player can now hop between Tier-1 points across most of
the map without touching the deck highway — low-tier fights exist.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 3 — MID-CANOPY BOUGHS (the 5-7m organic layer)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
From the titan trunk, grow 2 horizontal boughs (this is THE upgrade
that makes the treehouse feel like a tree):
  - Bough = tapered cyl (r 0.5→0.3, len 8-10m, axis x or z) at
    Y 5.2 and 6.4, exiting opposite sides.
  - Top surface made walkable with a 1.2m-wide foliage slab
    (GREEN, walkable) along the upper edge.
  - Ends in a 5m "leaf balcony" (green slab + skirt cone below it).
  - From balcony A: grapple ring chain (3 rings, +2.5m each) rises
    toward the NW bastion deck. From balcony B: a root ramp
    (wedge, 20° slope) descends to a Tier-1 boulder shelf.
Do the same mini-version on the SW gnarled tree (one bough at 4.5m).
Now the trunk has activity at 0 / 5-7 / 9.5 / 28 — the tree is a
vertical MAP, not an elevator.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 4 — GRAPPLE CHAINS, NOT RINGS (fix the network topology)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Current rings are mostly isolated. A ring is only useful if the
NEXT ring is visible and reachable from the swing. Rules:
  - Same-height chain: max horizontal gap 12-14m.
  - Ascending chain: gap 8-10m, +2 to 3m height per ring (matches
    swing-release arcs).
  - Every ring must have its successor within a ~55-degree cone of
    the swing direction; verify by raycast (File 09 automates).
Missing links to add:
  - Bamboo lantern line (File 02 Kit 8 IS this chain): lanterns at
    Y=9 spaced 8m → grove gets its missing aerial layer.
  - Skeleton spine chain: apex ring at ~7.7 → add rings at 10.5
    and 13 stepping NW toward the west bastion. The bone tunnel
    becomes an aerial on-ramp.
  - Low river line: rings at Y=6 over the water at x=21, z=-20/-8/4
    → a "skimming the stream" low route that contrasts the 9.5m
    bridges. Falling = splash zone, not death: fun.
  - Titan crown: the 31m apex ring is unreachable from any chain
    (nearest below: 16m airways = 15m gap). Add 22m and 26.5m
    rings above the bastions (2 of them) so there is a full
    ground→31m chain. Reward: full-arena glide + the margin
    doodles from File 02 are up there.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 5 — BOUNCE MUSHROOMS (concept Tier-2 taxonomy, never built)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The concept lists "giant_umbrella_mushroom_platform — elevated
bounce/sniper perch". Build buildBounceMushroom:
  - stalk cyl (r0.5, h 2.6) + cap = squashed sphere (r 2.2, half
    height 0.9), GREEN top with ORANGE gill ring underneath,
    noCollide cap top; game-side: standing on cap = launch impulse
    (set velocity +Y ~9.5 → reaches ~4.5m) with squash animation
    (scale.y 0.6→1 over 0.25s — one L.animated entry).
Place 3:
  - Glade → south bastion shortcut (lands on ridge top tier).
  - East riverbank → east bridge (banks Tier-1 → 9.5 needs the
    boost; place cap on a 3m boulder shelf).
  - Bamboo edge → lantern chain start.
Bounces are directional-funnel geometry: put a "landing intention"
around each (rail or fronds) so players know where they'll go.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 6 — ONE-WAY DROPS & DROP ZONES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Going down must be FASTER than going up (it's the reward for height):
  - Mark 4 official drop edges: bastion inner corners, deck hatch
    rim (outside the railing), bridge ends.
  - Below each: a MOSS PILE (flat GREEN squashed sphere, 2.2m wide,
    walkable) = land soft, zero fall damage, tiny dust puff
    (File 05 animation). Land anywhere else from 9.5m = damage.
  - This creates legitimate "yeet off the deck to escape" gameplay
    and teaches the map's drop vocabulary in one try.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 7 — COUNTER-SNUIPING THE SKY HIGHWAY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Test from each bastion deck: how much ground can an anchored sniper
cover? N/S bastions see the whole N/S trails (50m+ lanes). Fixes:
  - Needle-skirt curtains: on the 2 interior conifers nearest each
    bastion, drop the lowest skirt tier to 3m above ground (block
    the long lane without blocking movement).
  - Skim cover: 2 giant grass tufts repositioned 6-8m out from each
    bastion so the ground approach always has a final sprint gap.
  - The deck rail (0.95m) blocks crouched players — good; verify
    bots use the cover blocks on decks (they exist — keep them
    away from rail edges so bots don't silhouette).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 8 — THE 30-SECOND LOOP TEST (flow metric)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Define the core loop: SPAWN → reach deck (any route) → cross one
bridge → drop off → engage. Playtest with a timer:
  - Any leg > 12s = add a shortcut (bounce mushroom, root ramp, or
    extra ridge).
  - Count CHOICES at each junction: 1 choice = corridor, 2 = path,
    3+ = playground. The ascent to the deck should offer ≥3 routes
    from each spawn quadrant: ridge / stairs / Tier-1 hop-line /
    grapple chain / bounce. After this file: it does.
  - The "respawn loop": after dying on the deck, a respawned player
    should be back in the deck fight within 20s. That bound decides
    how many ascents each quadrant needs (answer: at least 2).
