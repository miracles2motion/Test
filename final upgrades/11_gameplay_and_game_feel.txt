PART 1 — BESTIARY: ASYMMETRY IS DIFFICULTY DESIGN
-------------------------------------------------
Your two forest enemies are well-differentiated on paper:

  TREE MONKEY  — rifle, range 26, keep 6, burst 3 @ 0.12s, dmg 5,
                 leaps, flanks, dodges, retreats, hp 85, speed 7.2
  BAMBOO STALKER — sniper, range 85, stop 85 (stands off!), keep 16,
                 aimTime 1.4s, dmg 22, spread 0.006, hp 65, speed 5.2

That is a REAL design: pressure vs punishment. Push it further:

  1. TELEGRAPHS IN INK (readability = fairness)
     - Stalker's 1.4s aim must be VISIBLE: a thin RED ruled laser
       line that draws itself along its aim ray (your RED = danger
       ink semantics, diegetic). Aim line locks -> shot lands
       where the line pointed 0.25s LATER (grace dodge window).
       Damage 22 with spread 0.006 at 85m is a near-hitscan; the
       telegraph is what makes it fun instead of cheap.
     - Monkey burst: 3 muzzle scribble flashes (tiny BLACK starbursts,
       File 06 U7 geometry) + audible tik-tik-tik (File 12).
       Players should read WHAT hit them without a UI.

  2. ROLE SPECIALIZATION BY SECTOR (the map teaches the enemy)
     - Monkeys never spawn in bamboo (can't leap between culms
       believably?) — no: LET them, but bamboo negates flanking
       (culms block) so they switch to ambush-from-above: drop
       from lantern height. Same stats, different BEHAVIOR FLAG
       per sector: monkey.aggressionMode = 'canopy' inside
       sector:bamboo. The sector system (File 07) gains
       enemyPosture per sector — Dream inherits real tactical
       variety for free.
     - Stalkers own long lanes: place 2-3 at perimeter conifer
       mid-heights overlooking N-S lanes (they punish the exact
       open lane File 04 Rule 7 complains about — the map's flaw
       becomes its teaching moment).

  3. THE DODGE/COVER LOOP (already spec'd: canDodge/canCover/
     canRetreat) — verify with a behavior LOG, not vibes: run a
     60s sim, log % time in each state. Healthy: <35% shooting,
     >20% repositioning. If a monkey never retreats, its Retreat
     flag is decorative — add a trigger (hp<40% AND reload AND
     cover within 8m).

  4. ESCALATION FOR SOLO WAVES (concept: treehouse ascent waves)
     Wave grammar: intro (monkeys only) -> mix + first stalker at
     wave 3 -> canopy monkeys wave 5 -> stalker PAIR with crossing
     lanes wave 6 -> alpha monkey (scale 1.15, hp x3, no retreat,
     drum burst) on the DECK at wave 8. Every 3rd wave drops ammo
     at the altar (pulls players through the bamboo — map tour by
     economy). Never spawn mid-combat within 12m of the player
     (spawn safety rule, File 08 V5 — reuse it for actors).

PART 2 — HIT FEEL IN A PAPER WORLD
----------------------------------
Your style forbids normal juice (no sparks, no blood) — so invent
paper juice. Every strike below is cheap in your pipeline:

  - INK SPLATS on surfaces (File 06 U7) — implemented? then hits
    already feel 2x better. Add the KILL version: 1.4m starburst
    + 3 droplet actors with gravity, page remembers for 6s.
  - HITSTOP: 40-60ms time-scale dip (0.15x) on kill, 25ms on
    headshot-hit. One variable in your loop: dt *= timeScale.
    Costs nothing, changes everything.
  - THE PEN PRESSES (screen response without camera shake nausea):
    on player damage — instead of generic shake, spike a new
    uniform uScribble: for ~0.3s the post pass adds hand-drawn
    jitter to line positions (wob amplitude = uScribble * noise).
    The WORLD visibly redraws itself shakier — the paper is
    nervous. This is the kind of idea only YOUR pipeline can do;
    it reads as style, not effect.
  - DAMAGE NUMBERS AS PENCIL SCRAWL: tiny canvas-textured quads,
    0.4s float, graphite ink, slight rotation — diegetic, no HUD
    numbers needed. Cap 8 live.
  - PICKUP MOMENT: page-white flash (uFlash exists!) at 0.35 +
    a RED circle drawn around the player position in world space
    (reuse File 06 U2 ring shader — instant). The game literally
    circles the moment like a grader's pen. Signature move.

PART 3 — THE PICKUP ECONOMY (currently 7 pickups, all free)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Rules that make pickups MEAN something:
  - Value vs risk: legendary in the cabin (highest ground, exposed
    approach) is correct. Armor shard in the skeleton belly (dark,
    enclosed, campable) — add a monkey pod nearby so it is contested.
  - Respawn cadence: health 20s, ammo 15s, legendary 45s AND
    announced (map-wide RED ripple at its ring — File 06 U2 with a
    bigger radius for 3s). Contested objectives create fights:
    the map already has sightlines to the cabin — now it has a
    REASON to watch them.
  - No pickup within 6m of a spawn (V5 extension).
  - Vertical pickups (banyan 12m, log roof 4m) reward the File 04
    vertical grammar — they are the tutorial that teaches Tier 1
    exists. Place 1 ammo on a Tier-1 boulder shelf.

PART 4 — SPAWN LOGIC & FLOW (solo + arena)
------------------------------------------
- The 94m open N-S spawn lane (File 04 Rule 7): fix with geometry
  AND logic — spawn selection must reject any spawn with LOS to a
  live enemy (ray, spatial hash) and prefer spawns near < 40% of
  the desired fight tier height for progression waves.
- Wave spawns in glades (concept's 8 solo spawns): use G2 cluster
  scatter with field masks so pods arrive FROM sectors, walking in
  through their sector's movement style (monkeys leap the berms,
  stalkers stalk the lanes). Enemies should ENTER like they live
  there — it sells the world more than any prop.

PART 5 — ACCESSIBILITY (the invisible masterpiece layer)
--------------------------------------------------------
- Your semantics are COLOR (5 inks). Add redundancy: RED danger
  elements also carry a scribble texture (uFill pattern or a glyph
  decal), BLUE water always animates (already will, File 06 U4) —
  a deuteranope player must be able to read danger/safe without
  hue. Cost: one pattern in the post shader.
- Settings: the graphics presets exist — surface them + add:
  camera FOV (80 base — offer 70-100), screenshake/scribble
  intensity 0-100%, hitstop toggle, damage numbers toggle.
  Every toggle is 3 lines and a lifetime of goodwill.
- Colorblind preview: a dev-only post toggle that desaturates inks
  to grayscale EXCEPT structure — screenshot it each release.
