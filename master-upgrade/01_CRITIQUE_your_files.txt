FILE 4: spline_engine.js (286 lines)  —  YOUR BEST FILE (with real bugs)
=====================================================================
Verdict: Technically competent and genuinely the right approach.
CatmullRomCurve3 + Frenet frames + lofting is how you should build
organic forms. KEEP THIS FILE. But it has four real problems:

  1) FRENET FRAME FLIPPING (the classic bug):
     computeFrenetFrames() fails wherever the curve has an inflection
     or straightens — the normal can flip 180°, twisting the tube into
     a self-intersecting mess. You have no guard for it. Fix: use a
     rotation-minimizing frame (parallel transport) or THREE's
     TubeGeometry which already handles this. (Batch 8 has the fix.)

  2) COLLIDERS ARE AXIS-ALIGNED BOXES:
     collider(mid.x, floorY, mid.z, floorW, 0.4, segLen*1.1, ...)
     These boxes are never ROTATED to follow the tangent. On a curved
     or diagonal tube, they stair-step and snag the player's feet.
     Fix: oriented capsules/cylinders per segment, aligned to tangent.

  3) NO UVs, NO MATERIAL, NO OUTLINE:
     The lofted tube is pushed through addGeo(geom, ink) which is a
     flat color. It will look like a plastic pipe, not a log/creature.
     Fix: the ink treatment from Batch 2 + 3 must apply here too.

  4) THE "HOLLOW FLOOR" IS A MAGIC NUMBER:
     floorY = mid.y - rMid * 0.55;   // arbitrary
     This is not a real tunnel cross-section. A real tunnel floor is a
     CHORD cut across the circle at a chosen height, leaving a flat
     walkable base. (Batch 8 gives the proper math.)

  5) radiusProfile interpolation is NEAREST-NEIGHBOR (jumps):
     idx = floor(t * (len-1))  -> the radius snaps instead of easing.
     Fix: linear/eased interpolation between profile samples.
