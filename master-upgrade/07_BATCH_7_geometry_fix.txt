=====================================================================
BATCH 7 — GEOMETRY & SILHOUETTE: KILL THE BOXES FOR REAL
=====================================================================
Time: ~4–6 hours (the biggest geometry batch). Now that rendering is
fixed, the remaining ugliness is SHAPE. Your research brags about
"eliminating box stacking," but the code still stacks boxes. This batch
fixes the specific offenders, in priority order.

GOLDEN PRINCIPLE:
  Silhouette first (Rule 5). Every object must read from 20m by
  outline. Bevel + taper + irregularity are your friends. A tiny
  bevel on a box is worth more than 10 extra boxes.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
FIX 1 — THE PERIMETER WALL (biggest single "cheap box arena" tell)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Current: four giant flat black boxes 20m tall. This is what makes it
look like a cardboard arena.

Change it to a VARIED tree-line silhouette, not a flat wall:
  - Instead of one flat box per side, place a ROW of big conifers /
    trunks with GAPS, so the edge is an irregular skyline (silhouette
    Rule 5).
  - Lower the "wall" to a low berm/boulder ridge (1–3m) for gameplay
    bounds, and let tall trees do the visual enclosure.
  - If you must keep a solid wall, at least: give it taper (wider at
    base), a slight random lean per segment, and a top "canopy" of
    overlapping leaf shapes instead of a flat box top.

Result: the edge of the map reads "forest" instead of "shipping crate."

---------------------------------------------------------------------
FIX 2 — THE GROUND (one flat green box = dead scene)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Current: box(0,-1,0, 2P+T, 1.0, 2P+T, GREEN). One color, zero life.

  - Add gentle HEIGHT variation. Keep the floor a single collision
    plane (so movement stays simple), but add VISUAL undulation:
    low mounds, moss humps, a slight basin around the river. Use a
    simple PlaneGeometry with a few displaced vertices, or scatter
    low "moss dome" caps.
  - BREAK UP THE COLOR: scatter large, very flat "moss patch" and
    "dirt path" decals (circles/ellipses, slightly above the floor).
    This kills the "one flat color" look for cheap.
  - A dirt path from each spawn to the center gives eye-lines and
    composition for free.

Result: the floor stops reading as "green plane."

---------------------------------------------------------------------
FIX 3 — THE RIVER (a 5cm blue slab is not a river)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Current: slab(...0.05 thick...BLUE). Blue strip.

  - Make it a slightly SUNKEN channel (lower the blue surface a touch,
    add dark bank edges on both sides) so it reads as a gully, not a
    sticker on the floor.
  - Give the water surface the drawn-contour material from Batch 4
    (translucent + wavy pen lines).
  - Add a few mossy stepping stones (irregular, beveled) across it.

Result: water reads as water, not blue tape.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
FIX 4 — SKYBRIDGES & DECKS (flat orange bars in the sky)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Current: slab() + rail(). Flat plank-less bars.

  - Add PLANK BREAKS: instead of one slab, several narrow planks with
    tiny gaps, plus two rope/rail lines (cylinders) with a slight
    SAG (a gentle curve, not a straight line).
  - Rails should be ROUND (cyl), not flat boxes.
  - Slight downward sag (2–3%) on every bridge sells weight.

Result: bridges read as rope bridges, not floating rulers.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
FIX 5 — TERRAIN STEPS (your "terraced ridge" is stairs)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Current: stacked slabs = a staircase wearing a hat.

  - Make each tier an IRREGULAR rock ledge: beveled, uneven edge,
    slightly different height, small boulders at the joints.
  - Give tiers a NATURAL rhythm (not uniform 1.6m steps — vary them).
  - Or replace with a single ramped boulder slope the player can
    mantle, with a few distinct handholds.

Result: ascent reads as rock, not architecture. (This is literally what
your concept doc asked for and the code failed to deliver.)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
FIX 6 — TREES (pines are currently cones-on-sticks, probably)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Since prefabs.js wasn't uploaded, assume they're cones/cylinders and
fix accordingly:
  - TRUNK: taper it (wider base -> narrower top). A cylinder is a pole;
    a tapered cylinder is a tree.
  - BARK: add a few vertical ridges (thin boxes/cylinders along the
    trunk) — with your toon shader + outline these read as bark lines.
  - CANOPY: use 3–5 overlapping cone/lens layers with slightly
    different sizes and a slight lean each, NOT one perfect cone.
    Perfect cones = christmas tree. Overlapping irregular layers =
    pine.
  - Add a slight RANDOM lean (2–5 degrees) and per-tree scale jitter.

Result: trees read as living trees by silhouette.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
FIX 7 — BAMBOO (green poles = pipe organ)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Your concept promised "leaf fan umbrellas" and "radiating fronds."
The code has none. Add them:
  - At the top of each culm, 3–5 flat leaf blades (thin ellipses)
    radiating outward and slightly drooping.
  - At each node ring, a tiny leaf tuft.
  - Slight per-culm lean and color jitter (2–3 greens).

Result: bamboo grove reads as a grove, not a grid of poles.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
FIX 8 — THE BONES / FISH / RIBBONS (primitives with grand names)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Bones: taper them (cone-like), add slight S-curves and joints,
    irregular knobs. A ribcage of straight cylinders reads as "bars."
  - Fish: your splineTube body is the right idea; give it a proper
    tail taper (radiusProfile already supports this — use it) and make
    the fins overlap the body (they currently float separately).
  - Ribbons (fins/leaves): these are the RIGHT primitive for pen art.
    Use more of them, everywhere — they're cheap and look hand-drawn.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
THE OVERARCHING RULE (memorize this)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Every object = 1 clear silhouette + 1 bevel + 1 irregularity.
  Stop adding objects to fix a scene; fix the SHAPES you have first.
  Less geometry, better silhouette, always beats more boxes.
